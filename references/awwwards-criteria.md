# Awwwards Criteria

## The Four Evaluation Dimensions

Awwwards judges sites on four dimensions. To win Site of the Day (SOTD), you need 8.5+ on ALL four. A beautiful site with poor usability won't win.

### 1. Design (8.5+ for SOTD)

Visual aesthetics, composition, color, typography, imagery.

**What wins:**
- Distinctive typography (not Inter/Roboto as primary display)
- Considered color palette with intention — not random
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Consistent spacing system
- Custom imagery, not stock photos
- Texture and atmosphere (grain, gradients, depth)
- No generic template look

**What kills:**
- Default Bootstrap/Tailwind look without customization
- Stock photos that look like stock photos
- Inconsistent spacing
- Pure black (#000) backgrounds
- System fonts as primary typography
- Random color choices without a system

### 2. Usability (8.5+ for SOTD)

Navigation, accessibility, responsiveness, intuitive UX.

**What wins:**
- Clear navigation path through the site
- Mobile experience is reconsidered, not just resized
- All interactive elements have visible hover/focus states
- Fast, responsive interactions
- Intuitive content structure
- Accessible (keyboard nav, screen reader, reduced motion)

**What kills:**
- Experimental navigation that requires discovery
- Scroll hijacking on text-heavy content
- Content hidden behind interactions
- Slow page transitions
- No mobile menu
- Tiny touch targets on mobile

### 3. Creativity (8.5+ for SOTD)

Innovation, uniqueness, memorable moments.

**What wins:**
- One signature unforgettable interaction
- Unexpected but purposeful design choices
- A unique visual identity
- Innovative use of technology
- Personalized or generative elements
- Storytelling through interaction

**What kills:**
- Copying another site's effect without adding your spin
- Effects without purpose (animation for animation's sake)
- No memorable moment
- Looking like every other site in the same industry

### 4. Content (8.5+ for SOTD)

Quality, storytelling, relevance, engagement.

**What wins:**
- Real, genuine content (not lorem ipsum)
- Content that unfolds with purpose and pacing
- Storytelling through scroll
- Photography that serves the narrative
- Microcopy that shows personality
- Content hierarchy that guides attention

**What kills:**
- Lorem ipsum
- Generic corporate copy
- Content as afterthought
- Text walls without visual breaks
- Stock imagery without context

## Scoring

- **Site of the Day (SOTD)**: 8.5+ across all four dimensions
- **Honorable Mention**: Strong in 2-3 dimensions, slightly below in others
- **Site of the Month**: Among the best SOTDs in a given month
- **Site of the Year**: The best of the best

## What Actually Wins in 2026

Based on analysis of 2026 SOTD winners:

### The Immersive Shift
61% of SOTD winners in Q1 2026 were immersive 3D experiences, up from 23% in the same period of 2024. Immersive sites outscore traditional builds on all four dimensions, with the largest gap in creativity scores (8.7 vs 6.4).

### Scroll-Driven Narrative
Scroll-driven 3D narratives outscore static 3D showcases by an average of 1.8 points. The winning approach: a master timeline controlled by normalized scroll position (0 to 1), with camera keyframes, material transitions, and content reveals mapped to specific progress thresholds.

### Design-Engineering Convergence
The sites winning in 2026 are built by people who design in the browser and engineer in the design tool. The interactions that win — magnetic cursors, kinetic type, scroll-bound video, GSAP timelines gated to viewport progress, WebGL hero sections — cannot be designed in Figma. They must be felt in the browser at production frame rate.

### Motion as the Deliverable
Motion is no longer the polish layer. It IS the deliverable. The hierarchy of attention is set by which element moves, not which element is biggest.

## The 8+ Site Checklist

An 8+ site has:
1. One signature unforgettable interaction
2. Cross-device parity where mobile is reconsidered, not merely responsive
3. Complex visuals that load fast on mid-range devices
4. Real content with genuine photography
5. Scroll as narrative, content unfolding with purpose and pacing
6. Precise animation choreography in timing, easing, and sequencing

## Anti-Patterns That Kill Scores

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|--------------|--------------|
| Generic chatbot widget | Obscures content, adds weight |
| Static gradient as primary design | No longer differentiated |
| Experimental navigation requiring discovery | Kills usability scores |
| Scroll animations that pause | Users think they reached the end |
| Heavy parallax as primary effect | Performance drag in 2026 |
| Bento without hierarchy | Reached oversaturation |
| WebGL on every site | Performance cost without brand justification |
| Kinetic typography everywhere | Should be hero-only |
| Glassmorphism on everything | Should be navigation/modals only |
| Motion as decoration | Motion must be communication |

## Submission Tips

1. **Submit on Tuesday-Thursday** — fewer submissions, better visibility
2. **Ensure the site is live and fully functional** — no "coming soon" sections
3. **Test on a mid-range Android device** — judges test on various devices
4. **Have real content** — no placeholder text or images
5. **Optimize loading speed** — judges have limited patience
6. **Include a case study** — describe your process, tools, and challenges
7. **Mobile must be excellent** — not just "responsive" but genuinely good
