## Sunday
- **URL:** https://www.sunday.ai/
- **Award:** SOTD Dec 31, 2025
- **Creator:** Standard Projects
- **Colors:** `#1A1A1A` (dark), `#F7E731` (yellow accent)
- **Fonts:** Friendly rounded sans-serif
- **Layout:** Immersive product storytelling, full-bleed sections
- **Animations:** Character mascot animation, scroll-driven product reveals, interactive elements
- **What Makes It Special:** Product mascot (Memo) drives the entire experience. Charm and personality without sacrificing usability.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - Character-driven product storytelling
  - Yellow-on-dark duotone (`#1A1A1A` + `#F7E731`)
  - Scroll-driven product feature reveals
  - Personality in micro-interactions

```css
/* Sunday's duotone palette */
:root {
  --bg: #1A1A1A;
  --accent: #F7E731;
  --text: #FFFFFF;
}
```

## Adaline
- **URL:** https://www.adaline.ai/
- **Award:** SOTD Jul 31, 2025
- **Creator:** Daybreak Studio
- **Colors:** `#B1CC7A` (single color — sage green)
- **Fonts:** Calm, readable sans-serif
- **Layout:** Landscape-inspired, flowing sections
- **Animations:** Subtle, calm motion — no aggressive effects
- **What Makes It Special:** Single-color palette. The site reflects the nature of the tool: living between language and logic, composed for clarity and calm.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - Single-color design system
  - Calm, restrained motion
  - Nature-inspired layout metaphors
  - Clarity over spectacle
