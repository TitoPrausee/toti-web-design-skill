# Experimental References — Award-Winning Sites

Sites analyzed from awwwards.com winners in the Experimental category.

---

## Lusion v3
- **URL:** https://lusion.com/
- **Award:** SOTY 2023 + Developer Award
- **Creator:** Lusion
- **Colors:** Dark with vivid WebGL-rendered colors
- **Fonts:** Custom, experimental
- **Layout:** Non-traditional, immersive 3D environment
- **Animations:** WebGL everything — 3D scenes, particle systems, shader effects, physics
- **What Makes It Special:** Won Site of the Year 2023. Pushes the boundaries of what's possible in a browser. Pure WebGL experience.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - Full WebGL environment design
  - 3D scene composition for web
  - Shader-based visual effects
  - Physics-based interactions
  - Performance optimization for heavy WebGL

## Igloo Inc
- **URL:** https://igloo.inc/
- **Award:** SOTY 2024 + Developer Award + Business Honors
- **Creator:** abeto
- **Colors:** Bold, playful palette
- **Layout:** Interactive, game-like experience
- **Animations:** Custom cursor, scroll-driven scenes, playful micro-interactions
- **What Makes It Special:** Won Site of the Year 2024. Playful, experimental, yet usable. Proves that experimental doesn't mean inaccessible.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - Playful experimental design
  - Game-like interactions
  - Custom cursor experiences
  - Accessibility in experimental design

## Breaking
- **URL:** https://www.breaking.com/
- **Award:** SOTD Aug 14, 2024
- **Creator:** Maven Creative, The Limbo Society
- **Colors:** `#2D41D3` (blue), `#FFFFFF` (white) — 2-color duotone
- **Fonts:** Clean sans-serif
- **Layout:** Immersive storytelling, scroll-driven chapters
- **Animations:** Scroll-driven content reveals, data visualization, parallax
- **What Makes It Special:** Plastic degradation science as engaging web experience. Educational content treated with award-winning design.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - 2-color duotone system (`#2D41D3` + `#FFFFFF`)
  - Educational storytelling through scroll
  - Data visualization as design
  - Science communication with visual excellence

## Don't Board Me
- **URL:** https://dontboardme.com/
