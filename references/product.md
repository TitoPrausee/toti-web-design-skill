## Lando Norris
- **URL:** https://landonorris.com/
- **Award:** SOTY 2025 + Developer Award
- **Creator:** OFF+BRAND
- **Colors:** #ff6b35 papaya orange, #0a0a0a dark, #f5f5f5 text
- **Fonts:** Bold display, racing aesthetic
- **Layout:** Immersive full-bleed, scroll-driven storytelling
- **Animations:** WebGL effects, scroll-driven transitions, high-performance motion
- **What Makes It Special:** Won Site of the Year 2025. Balances personality (racing driver brand) with performance. The motion feels fast without being jarring.
- **Techniques to Steal:**
  - Personal brand storytelling through scroll
  - Performance-optimized WebGL
  - Speed-themed motion design
  - Full-bleed immersive sections
